Research

I am currently working with Professor Daniel B. Cooney at the Univeristy of Illinois Urbana-Champaign.

I am currently exploring research questions related to Mathematical Biology. I am particularly interested in the analysis of ordinary and partial differential equation models (ODEs and PDEs) arising in biology, sociology, and economics, using methods from dynamical systems, stability theory, and bifurcation analysis.

Stay tuned for updates on my research progress!
